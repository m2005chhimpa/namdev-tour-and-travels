Before You Go

Useful Himachal travel information

Best time

March–June is popular for pleasant hill weather. Winter can be excellent for snow-focused trips; monsoon travel needs flexibility.

Getting there

Road travel is important for a multi-city circuit. Shimla, Bhuntar-Kullu and Kangra provide air access, with Chandigarh and Pathankot useful gateways.

Getting around

A private cab is convenient because mountain travel times, weather and sightseeing stops require flexibility.

Carry

  • Layered clothing
  • Comfortable shoes
  • Sun protection
  • Rain protection
