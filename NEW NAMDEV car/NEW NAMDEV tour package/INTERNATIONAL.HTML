Beach & City Circuit · Thailand

Thailand — Bangkok & Phuket Getaway

Thailand pairs two very different sides of Southeast Asia in one trip — Bangkok's gilded temples, street-food alleys, and rooftop skyline, followed by Phuket's turquoise bays and island-hopping. This circuit opens in Bangkok with the Grand Palace and river life along the Chao Phraya, then flies south to Phuket for beach time and a day trip out to the limestone cliffs of the Phi Phi Islands. It's one of the most accessible international trips for first-time overseas travelers from India — short flight time, visa on arrival for Indian passport holders, and a well-worn tourist infrastructure that makes getting around genuinely easy.

Direct 4hrsFlight Duration
6 DaysDuration
Visa on ArrivalEntry
Nov – FebBest Time

Very few international circuits let you combine a major capital city and a proper beach holiday in six days without feeling rushed — Bangkok and Phuket are close enough by air that you get both experiences in full rather than a compressed version of each.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Arrive in Bangkok, check in, evening at leisure exploring the riverside night markets.

Visit the Grand Palace and Wat Phra Kaew, cross the river to Wat Arun, evening free for shopping or a rooftop bar.

Morning flight to Phuket, check in, evening at Patong Beach.

Full-day boat trip to the Phi Phi Islands for snorkeling and Maya Bay, return to Phuket by evening.

Morning exploring Phuket's Sino-Portuguese Old Town, afternoon at leisure on the beach.

Transfer to Phuket airport for your flight home.

Places You'll Visit

Grand Palace, Bangkok

The former royal residence and Thailand's most iconic architectural landmark.

Phi Phi Islands

Limestone cliffs and turquoise water, best seen on a full-day boat trip from Phuket.

Phuket Old Town

Colourful Sino-Portuguese shophouses and a lively café and street-art scene.

City & Island Circuit · Malaysia

Malaysia — Kuala Lumpur & Langkawi

Malaysia offers one of the smoothest short-haul international trips from India, pairing Kuala Lumpur's futuristic skyline with the laid-back island of Langkawi. In KL, the Petronas Twin Towers and the limestone Batu Caves anchor a city that blends Malay, Chinese, and Indian influences into a genuinely distinct food and culture scene. Flying north to Langkawi swaps skyscrapers for beaches, mangroves, and the striking Sky Bridge suspended over the rainforest canopy. With Malaysia's visa-free entry for Indian e-passport holders and a short flight time, this is an easy five-day trip that doesn't feel rushed despite the short duration.

Direct 5hrsFlight Duration
5 DaysDuration
Visa-Free / eNTRIEntry
Dec – FebBest Time

Kuala Lumpur's night skyline from the Petronas Towers' observation deck is one of the more underrated city views in Southeast Asia, and pairing it with Langkawi's quieter island pace gives this short trip a nice contrast most five-day circuits don't manage.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Arrive, check in, evening visit to the Petronas Towers light show and KLCC Park.

Morning visit to Batu Caves, afternoon city tour covering Merdeka Square and Petaling Street.

Morning flight to Langkawi, check in, evening at Cenang Beach.

Cable car and Sky Bridge in the morning, afternoon island-hopping boat tour.

Transfer to Langkawi airport for your flight home.

Places You'll Visit

Petronas Twin Towers

Once the tallest buildings in the world, still Kuala Lumpur's defining skyline feature.

Batu Caves

A limestone hill temple complex reached by 272 brightly painted steps.

Langkawi Sky Bridge

A curved pedestrian bridge suspended above the rainforest canopy.

History & Balloons · Turkey

Turkey — Istanbul & Cappadocia

Turkey sits at the literal crossroads of Europe and Asia, and this circuit captures both the imperial history of Istanbul and the surreal volcanic landscape of Cappadocia. In Istanbul, the Hagia Sophia, Blue Mosque, and Topkapi Palace trace layers of Byzantine and Ottoman history within walking distance of each other, followed by a Bosphorus cruise between two continents. Flying inland to Cappadocia swaps domes and minarets for fairy chimney rock formations and underground cities, with a pre-dawn hot air balloon ride over the valley as the trip's signature moment — one of the most photographed sunrises anywhere in the world.

~9hrs (1 stop)Flight Duration
6 DaysDuration
e-VisaEntry
Apr – Jun, Sep – OctBest Time

Very few countries let you stand in a single city that has been the capital of two different empires, then fly two hours to a landscape carved by ancient volcanic eruptions — the contrast between Istanbul and Cappadocia is part of what makes this circuit so rewarding.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Arrive, check in, evening walk around Sultanahmet Square.

Full day covering Istanbul's three major historic sites within the old city.

Morning Bosphorus cruise between Europe and Asia, afternoon at the Grand Bazaar and Spice Market.

Morning flight to Cappadocia, afternoon exploring the Goreme Open Air Museum.

Pre-dawn hot air balloon ride over the valley, afternoon visit to an underground city and Uchisar Castle.

Fly back to Istanbul and onward for your flight home.

Places You'll Visit

Hagia Sophia

A Byzantine cathedral turned Ottoman mosque, nearly 1,500 years old.

Cappadocia Balloons

Hundreds of hot air balloons rising over the fairy chimney valleys at sunrise.

Bosphorus Strait

The waterway separating — and connecting — Istanbul's European and Asian sides.

City & Desert Circuit · UAE

Dubai & Abu Dhabi

Dubai remains one of the most convenient short international trips from India — a direct flight of around three hours into a city built around the Burj Khalifa, world-class shopping, and desert adventure just outside the urban sprawl. This circuit balances Dubai's skyline and a desert safari with camel rides and a Bedouin-style dinner, then extends to Abu Dhabi for the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, one of the most architecturally striking mosques in the world, and the Louvre Abu Dhabi. It's an easy, high-comfort international trip that works well for families, couples, and first-time overseas travelers alike.

Direct 3.5hrsFlight Duration
5 DaysDuration
Visa RequiredEntry
Nov – MarBest Time

The view from the Burj Khalifa's observation deck at sunset, followed by a desert safari under a full sky of stars the same evening, captures Dubai's strange and appealing contrast between hyper-modern city and open desert better than almost anything else on the itinerary.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Arrive, check in, evening at the Dubai Fountain and Dubai Mall.

Morning Burj Khalifa observation deck visit, afternoon city tour covering Jumeirah and the Palm.

Afternoon dune bashing, camel ride, and a Bedouin-style dinner with entertainment under the desert sky.

Full-day trip to Abu Dhabi covering the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque and Louvre Abu Dhabi.

Transfer to Dubai airport for your flight home.

Places You'll Visit

Burj Khalifa

The world's tallest building, with an observation deck offering sweeping desert-to-sea views.

Desert Safari

Dune bashing, camel rides, and a traditional dinner under the stars.

Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque

One of the largest and most architecturally striking mosques in the world.

Island Escape · Bali, Indonesia

Indonesia — Bali Escape

Bali remains one of the most consistently popular international getaways for Indian travelers, and for good reason — it packs temples, rice terraces, volcanic landscapes, and beaches into a single compact island. This circuit spends time in Ubud among the terraced rice paddies and the Sacred Monkey Forest, visits the clifftop Uluwatu Temple and the iconic sea-gate of Tanah Lot, takes a day trip out to Nusa Penida's dramatic coastline, and closes with beach time in Seminyak. With visa-on-arrival for Indian passport holders and a well-developed tourist circuit, it's an easy trip to plan despite the distance.

~9hrs (1 stop)Flight Duration
6 DaysDuration
Visa on ArrivalEntry
Apr – OctBest Time

Bali manages something few island destinations do — genuine cultural depth (temple ceremonies, traditional dance, the Hindu-influenced Balinese calendar) alongside postcard beaches, which is why travelers often come back for a second or third trip rather than treating it as a one-time box to tick.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Arrive, transfer to Ubud, evening at leisure.

Visit Tegallalang Rice Terrace and the Sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary, evening traditional dance performance.

Visit the sea-gate temple of Tanah Lot, afternoon at the clifftop Uluwatu Temple for sunset.

Full-day boat trip to Nusa Penida for its dramatic cliffside viewpoints and beaches.

Relaxed beach day in Seminyak, optional spa or surfing lesson.

Transfer to Denpasar airport for your flight home.

Places You'll Visit

Tegallalang Rice Terrace

Cascading emerald rice paddies just outside Ubud, one of Bali's most photographed views.

Uluwatu Temple

A clifftop temple perched 70 metres above the Indian Ocean, famous for sunset views.

Nusa Penida

Dramatic limestone cliffs and turquoise water off Bali's southeast coast.

City & Island Circuit · Singapore

Singapore — City & Sentosa

Singapore packs an extraordinary amount into a small footprint — futuristic gardens, a buzzing multicultural food scene, and a full-scale entertainment island, all connected by one of the most efficient transit systems in the world. This compact four-day circuit covers Marina Bay Sands and the Gardens by the Bay's Supertree domes, a full day at Sentosa Island for Universal Studios and the beaches, and a city tour through Chinatown, Little India, and the colonial district. It's an ideal short trip for families, and works equally well as a standalone getaway or a stopover extension on a longer Southeast Asia itinerary.

Direct 5.5hrsFlight Duration
4 DaysDuration
e-VisaEntry
Feb – Apr, Jul – SepBest Time

Singapore's Gardens by the Bay light show after dark, viewed from beneath the Supertree canopy, is a genuinely unique sight that photographs never quite capture — it's one of the few must-see attractions in Asia that's just as impressive the second time you see it.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Arrive, check in, evening at Marina Bay Sands and the Gardens by the Bay light show.

Full day at Sentosa — Universal Studios Singapore or the island's beaches and cable car, your choice.

Morning at Gardens by the Bay's domes, afternoon city tour through Chinatown, Little India, and the Merlion waterfront.

Transfer to Changi Airport for your flight home.

Places You'll Visit

Marina Bay Sands

Singapore's iconic three-tower hotel with its rooftop infinity pool and skyline views.

Gardens by the Bay

Futuristic Supertree structures and climate-controlled biodomes by the bay.

Sentosa Island

A dedicated resort island with theme parks, beaches, and a cable car connecting to the mainland.
