Before You Go

Good to know

Best time to visit

October to June generally offers pleasant to snowy conditions depending on what you're after. December to February brings snow, while March to June is greener and better for Rohtang Pass access. Monsoon months (July–September) can bring landslides on the higher routes.

Getting there

Manali is reachable by road via NH3 from Chandigarh or Delhi, by overnight Volvo buses, or by flying into Bhuntar (Kullu) airport and continuing by road for around 1.5–2 hours.

Getting around

Local taxis and hired cars cover most sightseeing points comfortably. A private vehicle is especially useful for higher-altitude stops like Rohtang Pass, which may need permits during peak season.

What to carry

  • Warm layers and a windproof jacket, even in summer.
  • Sturdy, closed shoes for walks near falls, lakes, and passes.
  • Sunscreen and sunglasses — sun is stronger at altitude.
  • A valid photo ID, sometimes needed for permits toward Rohtang Pass.
Taste & Explore

Don't leave Manali without experiencing the town.

The food, cafes, and mountain experiences are as much a part of Manali as its sightseeing spots.

🍲

Himachali Cuisine

Try siddu, dham thalis, and trout from the local rivers at family-run eateries around town.

☕

Old Manali Cafes

Riverside cafes with mountain views, live music evenings, and a relaxed backpacker atmosphere.

🛍️

Mall Road

Shop for pashmina shawls, woollens, and local handicrafts along Manali's main shopping stretch.

♨️

Vashisht Hot Springs

Natural sulphur hot springs near an old temple complex — a favorite after a day of sightseeing.

🪂

Solang Valley Adventure

Paragliding, zorbing, and (in season) snow activities in the valley just outside town.

🚣

River Rafting on the Beas

White-water rafting stretches near Kullu make for a popular half-day add-on to a Manali trip.
