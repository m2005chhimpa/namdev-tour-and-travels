Before You Go

Good to know

Best time to visit

October to March is the most comfortable window — Jodhpur sits on the edge of the Thar Desert, so summers (April–June) run very hot. If you're visiting Mehrangarh Fort in warmer months, plan for an early morning start.

How to reach Jodhpur

  • By air: Jodhpur Airport connects to Delhi, Mumbai, and other major cities.
  • By train: Jodhpur Junction is well-connected across Rajasthan and to Delhi.
  • By road: A common add-on from Jaipur or Udaipur — we can arrange the car either way.
While You're Here

What to eat & where to shop

🥟

Mawa Kachori

Jodhpur's signature sweet-savoury kachori, stuffed with a khoya (mawa) filling.

🥤

Makhaniya Lassi

A rich, buttery lassi topped with malai — a Jodhpur specialty best had chilled.

🌶️

Mirchi Bada

Large stuffed, batter-fried chillies — a favourite street-food snack in the old city.

🕰️

Clock Tower & Sardar Market

The heart of old Jodhpur — spices, textiles, and street food all around the tower.

🧣

Bandhej (Tie-Dye)

Rajasthan's traditional tie-dye fabric, widely sold across Jodhpur's markets.

👞

Mojari / Juttis

Handcrafted leather footwear with traditional embroidery — a classic Jodhpur souvenir.
