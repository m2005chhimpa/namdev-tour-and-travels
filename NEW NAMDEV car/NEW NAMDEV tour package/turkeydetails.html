"Turquoise Coast Cruise Package"
click here to ask price

Gulet or Yacht Cruise: Accommodation on a traditional wooden gulet or yacht, offering an intimate and authentic sailing experience. Various cabin options, from standard to luxury, depending on the package.
Coastal Destinations: Stops at picturesque coastal towns and villages along the Turquoise Coast, such as Antalya, Kas, Fethiye, and Bodrum. Exploration of historical sites, markets, and local culture during shore excursions.
Island-Hopping Excursions: Visits to idyllic islands, both inhabited and uninhabited, for swimming, snorkeling, and relaxation. Opportunities to explore historical ruins on some of the islands.
Crystal-Clear Waters: Swimming and water activities in the turquoise and azure waters of the Mediterranean and Aegean Seas. Snorkeling to discover vibrant underwater marine life. br Culinary Experiences: Onboard dining featuring delicious Turkish cuisine, including fresh seafood, mezes, and local specialties. Optional visits to seaside restaurants for a taste of regional flavors.
Historical and Cultural Exploration: Guided tours to ancient sites and historical landmarks along the coast. Visits to archaeological ruins, ancient theaters, and well-preserved cities. Sunset and Sunrise Views: Scenic cruises during sunrise and sunset, providing breathtaking views of the coastline and islands. Relaxation on deck with panoramic vistas.
Water Activities: Use of water sports equipment, such as kayaks, paddleboards, and inflatable rafts. Optional activities like water skiing, jet-skiing, or fishing.
Onboard Amenities: Comfortable cabins with ensuite facilities. Lounge areas, sun decks, and shaded spaces for relaxation. Professional crew and onboard services.
Flexible Itineraries: Customization of the cruise itinerary based on traveler preferences and the duration of the package. Options for private charters for a more personalized experience.

Day 1: Arrival in Antalya
Arrive in Antalya and transfer to the marina.
Welcome aboard the gulet or yacht.
Introduction to the crew and a briefing about the cruise.
Dinner on board and overnight in Antalya.

Day 2: Antalya to Kas
Morning cruise along the Turquoise Coast.
Visit the ancient city of Phaselis, known for its historical ruins.
Afternoon swim stops in secluded bays.
Continue sailing to Kas, a charming coastal town.
Explore the town, visit the local market, and dine at a seaside restaurant.

Day 3: Kas to Kekova
Cruise to Kekova, known for its sunken city ruins.
Swim and snorkel in the crystal-clear waters.
Explore Simena Castle and the Lycian necropolis.
Evening at leisure on board or in a local village.

Day 4: Kekova to Demre
Sail to Demre and visit the ancient city of Myra.
Explore the rock-cut tombs and the Church of St. Nicholas.
Afternoon swim stops in turquoise bays.
Dinner on board and overnight in Demre.

Day 5: Demre to Fethiye
Cruise to Fethiye, passing through Butterfly Valley.
Visit the mesmerizing Oludeniz Beach and Blue Lagoon.
Explore the vibrant town of Fethiye in the evening.

Day 6: Fethiye to Gocek
Sail to Gocek, a picturesque bay surrounded by pine-covered mountains.
Enjoy water activities, including snorkeling and paddleboarding.
Relax on board and soak in the tranquil surroundings.

Day 7: Gocek to Marmaris
Cruise to Marmaris, a bustling coastal town.

"Cappadocia Adventure Tour"
click here to ask price

Hot Air Balloon Ride: A signature experience allowing participants to soar above the otherworldly landscapes of Cappadocia, witnessing the fairy chimneys and rock formations during sunrise.
Hiking and Trekking: Exploration of the stunning valleys and rock-carved landscapes on foot. Guided hikes through places like Rose Valley, Love Valley, and Pigeon Valley, offering panoramic views and encounters with unique geological features.
Cave Dwelling Exploration: Visits to ancient cave dwellings, underground cities, and rock-cut churches. Exploration of Göreme Open-Air Museum, showcasing rock-cut churches with intricate frescoes.
ATV (All-Terrain Vehicle) Tours: Off-road adventure exploring the valleys and viewpoints on ATVs. Guided ATV tours providing an exhilarating way to discover the region's diverse terrain.
Underground City Tours: Exploration of Cappadocia's underground cities, such as Derinkuyu or Kaymaklı, which served as shelters and storage areas during ancient times.
Rock Climbing: Opportunities for rock climbing activities for varying skill levels, with Cappadocia's unique rock formations providing a natural climbing playground.
Horseback Riding: Scenic horseback rides through Cappadocia's valleys, offering a unique perspective of the landscape.
Options for both beginners and experienced riders. Quad Biking Adventures: Quad biking excursions allowing participants to navigate the rugged terrain and valleys, combining adventure with scenic views.
Traditional Turkish Night Show: Cultural experiences, including traditional Turkish night shows with folk music, dance performances, and local cuisine.
Balik Ekmek (Fish Sandwich) Boat Tour : Boat tour on the scenic Göreme Stream, often accompanied by a local culinary experience like enjoying Balik Ekmek, a popular fish sandwich.
Local Handicraft Workshops: Optional visits to local workshops where traditional crafts like pottery, carpet weaving, and tile making are practiced.

Day 1: Arrival in Cappadocia
Arrive in Cappadocia and transfer to your accommodation.
Evening orientation with the tour guide and a welcome dinner.
Overnight stay in a cave hotel.

Day 2: Hot Air Balloon Ride and Hiking in Rose Valley
Early morning hot air balloon ride to witness sunrise over Cappadocia.
Breakfast and rest at the hotel.
Guided hike in Rose Valley, exploring unique rock formations and cave churches.
Visit to the Göreme Open-Air Museum.
Dinner at a local restaurant.
Overnight stay in Cappadocia.

Day 3: ATV Adventure and Underground City Exploration
Morning ATV tour through the valleys and fairy chimneys.
Visit to an underground city, such as Derinkuyu or Kaymaklı, exploring the intricate tunnels and chambers.
Lunch at a local restaurant.
Afternoon free time for optional activities or relaxation.
Evening Turkish night show with traditional music and dance performances.
Overnight stay in Cappadocia.

Day 4: Horseback Riding and Zelve Open-Air Museum
Morning horseback riding excursion through the scenic landscapes.
Lunch at a local ranch or restaurant.
Visit to Zelve Open-Air Museum, known for its cave dwellings and rock-cut churches.
Optional visit to a local pottery workshop.
Dinner at a traditional Turkish restaurant.
Overnight stay in Cappadocia.

Day 5: Rock Climbing and Quad Biking Adventure
Morning rock climbing experience at a designated site.
Lunch at a local restaurant.
Afternoon quad biking adventure through valleys and trails.
Free time to explore the town or relax.
Farewell dinner at a special location.
Overnight stay in Cappadocia.

Day 6: Göreme Stream Boat Tour and Local Handicraft Workshops
Morning boat tour on the scenic Göreme Stream.
Enjoy a Balik Ekmek (Fish Sandwich) lunch on the boat.
Visit local handicraft workshops, such as pottery or carpet weaving.
Afternoon free time for shopping or personal exploration.
Evening farewell dinner with the group.
Overnight stay in Cappadocia.

Day 7: Departure
Check-out from the hotel and transfer to the airport for departure.

Cultural Immersion in Anatolia

Local Community Engagement: Interaction and engagement with local Anatolian communities to understand their traditions, daily life, and cultural practices. This might involve homestays, village visits, and participation in local activities or events.
Culinary Experiences: Sampling authentic Anatolian cuisine through cooking classes, visits to local markets, and dining at traditional restaurants. This includes trying regional dishes, spices, and specialties unique to Anatolia.
Arts, Crafts, and Workshops: Opportunities to learn about Anatolian arts and crafts such as pottery, carpet weaving, tile making, and traditional handcrafts. This could involve hands-on experiences or visits to artisan workshops.
Historical and Cultural Sites: Visits to historical landmarks, ancient ruins, and cultural sites that are integral to Anatolia's history. This may include exploring ancient cities, archaeological sites, museums, and monuments that showcase Anatolia's rich heritage.
Religious and Spiritual Exploration: Visits to religious sites and places of spiritual significance, reflecting Anatolia's diverse religious history, including mosques, churches, and temples.
Festivals and Celebrations: Participation in local festivals, cultural events, or traditional celebrations that highlight Anatolian customs, music, dance, and rituals.
Language and Traditions: Opportunities to learn about Anatolian languages, dialects, and traditional practices handed down through generations.
Rural and Urban Contrasts: Experiencing the contrast between rural Anatolian life and urban settings to gain a comprehensive understanding of the region's diversity and cultural dynamics.
Natural Wonders and Landscapes: Exploring Anatolia's diverse landscapes, including mountains, valleys, and fertile plains, which have shaped the region's history and cultural practices.
Local Guides and Experts: Guided tours by knowledgeable locals or experts who provide insights into Anatolian history, culture, and traditions, enhancing the immersive experience.

Day 1: Arrival in Ankara
Arrive in Ankara, the capital city of Turkey.
Transfer to the hotel and check-in.
Welcome orientation and introduction to the Anatolian culture.
Evening exploration of local neighborhoods and dinner at a traditional Turkish restaurant.

Day 2: Ankara City Exploration
Morning visit to the Museum of Anatolian Civilizations.
Guided tour of Ataturk's Mausoleum (Anitkabir) to learn about modern Turkish history.
Afternoon visit to Hamamonu district for a taste of local art and culture.
Evening free time for personal exploration or relaxation.

Day 3: Cappadocia and Cultural Workshops
Drive to Cappadocia, a region known for its unique landscapes.
Visit a local pottery workshop to experience traditional Anatolian crafts.
Explore the Göreme Open-Air Museum, showcasing cave churches with frescoes.
Evening cultural performance showcasing regional music and dance.

Day 4: Hot Air Balloon Ride and Valley Exploration
Early morning hot air balloon ride over the surreal landscapes of Cappadocia.
Breakfast and rest at the hotel.
Guided hike in Rose Valley, exploring ancient cave dwellings and rock formations.
Visit local carpet-weaving workshops to learn about traditional Anatolian art.

Day 5: Kayseri and Traditional Cooking Class
Travel to Kayseri, known for its historical sites and traditional markets.
Visit the Kayseri Castle and Erciyes Ski Resort (seasonal).
Afternoon traditional cooking class to learn Anatolian culinary techniques.
Dinner featuring dishes prepared during the cooking class.

Day 6: Konya and Sufi Whirling Dervishes
Drive to Konya, the city of Rumi, a renowned Sufi poet.
Visit the Mevlana Museum and Mausoleum.
Evening attendance at a Sufi Whirling Dervishes ceremony.
Overnight stay in Konya.

Day 7: Rural Village Experience
Visit a traditional Anatolian village.
Experience rural life with activities like bread-making, farming, or animal husbandry.
Lunch with a local family to savor authentic Anatolian cuisine.
Afternoon drive to Pamukkale for the next cultural experience.

Day 8: Pamukkale and Hierapolis
Explore the white terraces of Pamukkale, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
Visit the ancient city of Hierapolis and its well-preserved ruins.
Relax in the thermal pools with views of the surrounding landscape.
Evening free time for leisure or optional activities.

Day 9: Aphrodisias and Turkish Folk Music
Visit the ancient city of Aphrodisias, dedicated to the goddess Aphrodite.
Explore the well-preserved stadium, theater, and sculpture workshop.
Evening Turkish folk music performance with traditional instruments.
Farewell dinner featuring local specialties.

Day 10: Departure
Check-out from the hotel and transfer to the airport for departure.
