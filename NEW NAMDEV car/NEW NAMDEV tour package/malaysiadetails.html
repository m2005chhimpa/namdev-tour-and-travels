Classic Malaysia Tour Package
click here to Ask Price

Destinations: The package covers major tourist destinations in Malaysia, such as Kuala Lumpur (the capital city), Penang, and possibly other highlights like Melaka or Langkawi.
Activities: A mix of sightseeing, cultural experiences, and perhaps some free time for individual exploration. It may include visits to iconic landmarks, historical sites, cultural performances, and natural attractions.
Accommodations: Accommodations are usually included in the package, ranging from mid-range to upscale hotels based on the package type and customer preferences.
Transportation: Transportation arrangements within Malaysia, including airport transfers, inter-city travel, and possibly guided transportation for specific tours or activities.
Meals: Some packages may include meals, either as part of guided tours or at selected local restaurants to provide a taste of Malaysia's diverse cuisine.
Guides: Professional guides may be provided to offer insights, historical context, and assistance throughout the journey.


Day 1: Arrival in Kuala Lumpur
Arrive at Kuala Lumpur International Airport.
Transfer to the hotel for check-in.
Welcome briefing and free time to relax.
Optional evening exploration of Kuala Lumpur's city lights.

Day 2: Kuala Lumpur City Tour
Morning visit to the Petronas Twin Towers and KLCC Park.
Afternoon exploration of the National Museum and National Mosque.
Evening stroll in Bukit Bintang for shopping and dining.

Day 3: Melaka Day Trip
Day trip to the historic city of Melaka.
Explore Jonker Street, visit historical sites, and enjoy local cuisine.
Return to Kuala Lumpur in the evening.

Day 4: Cameron Highlands
Travel to Cameron Highlands, known for its cool climate and tea plantations.
Visit a tea plantation, explore the Mossy Forest, and enjoy the cool weather.
Overnight stay in Cameron Highlands.

Day 5: Penang Arrival
Journey to Penang, known for its rich cultural heritage.
Check-in to the hotel and relax.
Evening visit to Penang's bustling street markets.

Day 6: Penang City Tour
Explore George Town's UNESCO-listed heritage sites.
Visit the Khoo Kongsi Clan House and Penang Peranakan Mansion.
Relax at Batu Ferringhi Beach in the evening.

Day 7: Langkawi Island
Fly to Langkawi and check-in at the beach resort.
Afternoon at leisure to enjoy the beach or explore nearby attractions.
Sunset cruise or beachside dinner.

Day 8: Langkawi Island Hopping

Full-day island hopping tour, exploring Pulau Dayang Bunting, Pulau Singa Besar, and Pulau Beras Basah.
Water activities like snorkeling and swimming.
Free evening to relax.

Day 9: Departure

Morning at leisure for last-minute shopping or beach time.
Check-out and transfer to Langkawi International Airport for departure.

Adventure Islands: Visits to adventure-centric islands like Langkawi or Tioman, offering activities such as snorkeling, scuba diving, and water sports.
Canopy Walks: Experiencing the thrill of walking on elevated canopy walkways, often found in rainforest areas like Taman Negara.
Zip-lining: Adventure parks or natural settings that offer zip-lining experiences through lush landscapes.
Off-Road Adventures: 4x4 jungle expeditions or off-road biking experiences to explore remote and less accessible areas.
Homestays and Cultural Immersion: Opportunities to stay in local villages, interact with indigenous communities, and learn about traditional cultures and practices.
Adventure Resorts: Accommodations that cater to adventure travelers, providing a comfortable base for exploring nearby activities.

Free time to explore Kuala Lumpur.

Scenic Transfers: Scenic transfers between the rainforest and beach destinations, allowing travelers to appreciate the changing landscapes.
Beach Resort Stay: Arrival at a coastal destination known for its pristine beaches. Accommodations in luxury beach resorts with amenities such as private beaches, spa services, and water sports facilities.
Water Activities: Enjoying water-based activities like snorkeling, scuba diving, or kayaking in the clear waters of the beach. Optional boat tours or island-hopping excursions.
Relaxation and Leisure: Leisure time for beach relaxation, sunbathing, and enjoying the serene coastal environment. Spa treatments and wellness activities offered by beach resorts.
Culinary Experiences: Sampling local cuisine, seafood, and tropical fruits, both within the rainforest region and at the beach destination.
Flexibility in Itinerary:
A flexible itinerary that allows travelers to customize the balance between rainforest and beach experiences based on their preferences.
