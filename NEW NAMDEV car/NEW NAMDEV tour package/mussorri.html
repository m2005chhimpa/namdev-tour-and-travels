The Mussoorie Experience

A timeless hill station of mountain views, colonial charm and forested walks.

Mussoorie is one of Uttarakhand's most loved mountain escapes, rising along the Himalayan foothills above Dehradun. From Mall Road and Gun Hill to Kempty Falls, Lal Tibba, Landour and Camel's Back Road, the destination combines scenic viewpoints, waterfalls, heritage, cafés, peaceful walks and classic hill-station character.

Before You Go

Good to know

Best time to visit

March to June is popular for pleasant summer weather. Autumn often brings clearer mountain views, while winter is cold and can bring occasional snowfall. Monsoon can make hill roads wet and visibility changeable.

Getting there

Mussoorie is reached by road from Dehradun, which is the main gateway to the hill station. Dehradun Railway Station and Jolly Grant Airport provide convenient onward connections.

Getting around

Central Mussoorie is walkable in many places, while a private cab is more practical for Kempty Falls, Lal Tibba, Cloud's End, Company Garden and other attractions spread across the hills.

What to carry

  • Comfortable walking shoes for slopes and hill paths.
  • Warm layers, especially in evenings and winter.
  • Water, sun protection and a light rain layer.
  • Extra care on wet or steep paths during monsoon.
