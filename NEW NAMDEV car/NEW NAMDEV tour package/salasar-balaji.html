Before You Go

Good to know

Best time to visit

October to March is generally comfortable for exploring Shekhawati. Festival periods such as Holi can transform Salasar with huge crowds and special celebrations, so plan transport and accommodation well in advance.

How to reach Salasar

  • By air: Delhi is the most practical major-airport gateway for many visitors.
  • By train: Churu Heritage Junction is the main rail gateway, with Salasar a short road journey away.
  • By road: Salasar is easily combined with Churu Heritage, Agra and Delhi routes — we can arrange the car for the complete circuit.

Temple visit etiquette

Dress respectfully, follow photography restrictions where applicable and keep extra time around major temples. During peak festival periods, walking can be easier than trying to take a car directly through crowded lanes.

Plan your timing

Temple schedules and crowd conditions can vary by day and festival. We recommend confirming the current darshan schedule before finalising your route, especially for special occasions.
