Frequently Asked Question for this Pack



What is the best time to visit Dubai ?
Dubai is most enjoyable during the winter months, from November to March, when the weather is cooler.

What is the currency in Dubai?
The currency used in Dubai is the United Arab Emirates Dirham (AED).

Do I need a visa to visit Dubai?
Depending on your nationality, you may need a visa to enter Dubai. Check the visa requirements before traveling.

What is the official language in Dubai?
Arabic is the official language, but English is widely spoken and understood.

Is it safe to travel to Dubai?
Dubai is generally considered a safe destination for tourists. However, it's always wise to practice common travel safety precautions.

What is the dress code in Dubai?
While Dubai is modern and cosmopolitan, it's respectful to dress modestly in public areas. Swimwear is acceptable at beaches and pools.

Can I drink alcohol in Dubai?
Alcohol is available in licensed venues like hotels and clubs. However, public intoxication is not tolerated.

What are the must-visit attractions in Dubai?
Popular attractions include the Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall, Burj Al Arab, Palm Jumeirah, and the Dubai Fountain.

How is the transportation system in Dubai?
Dubai has a well-developed public transportation system, including the metro, buses, and taxis. Renting a car is also a common option.

Is it necessary to tip in Dubai?
Tipping is appreciated but not obligatory. A service charge is often included in bills at hotels and restaurants.

Are there any cultural norms I should be aware of?
Respect local customs and traditions, such as avoiding public displays of affection and dressing modestly in religious areas.

What is the voltage and plug type in Dubai?
The standard voltage is 230V, and the plug type is the British-style three-pin plug.

How can I stay connected to the internet in Dubai?
Dubai has widespread Wi-Fi coverage, and you can also purchase local SIM cards for data access.

What are the shopping hours in Dubai?
Malls generally open from 10 am to 10 pm, while smaller shops may close for a few hours in the afternoon.

Are there any specific health precautions I should take?
Tap water is safe to drink, but it's advisable to drink bottled water. Also, be aware of the strong sun, so use sunscreen and stay hydrated.
