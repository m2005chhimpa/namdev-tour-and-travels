Before You Go

Good to know

Best time to visit

October to March is by far the most comfortable window — daytime temperatures stay pleasant and desert nights are cool but manageable. Summers regularly cross 45°C and are best avoided.

Getting there

Jaisalmer has a small airport with limited seasonal flights, plus a well-connected railway station with direct trains from Jodhpur, Jaipur, and Delhi. It's also an easy road trip from Jodhpur (~5-6 hrs) or Jaipur (~9-10 hrs).

Getting around

The fort and old city are best explored on foot, but a private cab is the practical choice for reaching Sam Sand Dunes and other spots spread across the desert outskirts of town.

What to carry

  • Sunscreen, sunglasses, and a hat — the desert sun is intense year-round.
  • Light cotton clothing for the day, a warm layer for chilly desert nights.
  • A scarf or bandana to keep dust off during camel rides and dune visits.
  • Comfortable, closed walking shoes for the fort's uneven stone lanes.
Taste & Explore

What to eat & where to shop

The Golden City's food and bazaars are as much a part of the visit as its monuments.

🍛

Dal Baati Churma

Rajasthan's signature meal — baked wheat rolls, spiced lentils, and sweet crumbled churma — widely available across the old city.

🍯

Ghevar & Local Sweets

Look for ghevar and other Rajasthani sweets at old-city shops, especially around festival season.

🧵

Sadar Bazaar

The main market for mirror-work textiles, embroidered bags, and Rajasthani jootis — expect to bargain.

🎭

Puppets & Handicrafts

Traditional Rajasthani string puppets (kathputli) and carved wooden souvenirs make distinctive, packable gifts.

🔥

Desert Camp Dinner

An evening thali with live folk music and dance at the Sam Dunes camp is a highlight most travelers remember long after the trip.

🏙️

Rooftop Cafés

Several rooftop restaurants inside the old city offer fort views over dinner — a relaxed way to end the day.
