A Suggested 3-Day Itinerary

A simple way to see the best of Rishikesh without rushing — swap days around to fit your pace.

DAY
1
Arrival — Evening

Bridges, Ghats & the Ganga Aarti

Check in, then walk across Laxman Jhula and Ram Jhula. Explore Swarg Ashram's lanes, and end the day at Triveni Ghat for the evening Ganga Aarti — one of Rishikesh's most moving experiences.

DAY
2
Full Day — Adventure

Waterfalls & Whitewater

Morning white water rafting on the Ganges, followed by a visit to Neergarh or Patna Waterfall to cool off. Evening free to explore cafes along the riverside.

DAY
3
Morning — Departure

Temples, Ashrams & Beatles Ashram

Visit Neelkanth Mahadev Temple or Swami Dayananda Ashram, then wander the graffiti-covered halls of the Beatles Ashram before checkout and departure.

Local Flavor

Where to Eat

Rishikesh is entirely vegetarian and alcohol-free — expect fresh, wholesome food with a strong café culture along the river.

Take It Home

Where to Shop

From spiritual keepsakes to yoga essentials, Rishikesh's lanes are full of small finds worth bringing home.
