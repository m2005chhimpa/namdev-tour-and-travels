A Suggested 3-Day Itinerary

A simple way to see the best of Kevadia without rushing — swap days around to fit your pace.

DAY
1
Arrival — Evening

The Statue of Unity & EKTA Cruise

Check in, then head to the viewing gallery at 400ft up the statue, followed by the museum and exhibition inside. In the evening, take the EKTA Dinner Cruise on the Narmada for a lit-up view of the statue.

DAY
2
Full Day — Adventure

Rafting, Waterfalls & the Zoo

Morning river rafting and a Burma Bridge selfie stop at Khalwani, an afternoon trek to Zarwani Waterfall, then an evening jungle safari at Sardar Patel Zoological Park.

DAY
3
Morning — Departure

Gardens & Panchmuli Lake

Wander Arogya Van and Vishwa Van's themed gardens, then a peaceful boat ride at Panchmuli Lake before checkout and departure.

Local Flavor

Where to Eat

Kevadia's dining options are centered around the Statue of Unity complex, with a mix of Gujarati thalis and food courts.

Take It Home

Where to Shop

Souvenirs and local handicrafts are available around the statue complex and shopping center.
