Before You Go

Good to know

Best time to visit

October to March is generally comfortable for exploring Shekhawati. Festival periods such as Holi can transform Khatu Shyam Ji with huge crowds and special celebrations, so plan transport and accommodation well in advance.

How to reach Khatu Shyam Ji

  • By air: Delhi is the most practical major-airport gateway for many visitors.
  • By train: Shekhawati Heritage Junction is the main rail gateway, with Khatu Shyam Ji a short road journey away.
  • By road: Khatu Shyam Ji is easily combined with Shekhawati Heritage, Agra and Delhi routes — we can arrange the car for the complete circuit.

Temple visit etiquette

Dress respectfully, follow photography restrictions where applicable and keep extra time around major temples. During peak festival periods, walking can be easier than trying to take a car directly through crowded lanes.

Plan your timing

Temple schedules and crowd conditions can vary by day and festival. We recommend confirming the current darshan schedule before finalising your route, especially for special occasions.
