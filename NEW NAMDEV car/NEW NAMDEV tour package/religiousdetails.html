Char Dham Yatra
click here to Ask Price


Yamunotri: Located in the Garhwal Himalayas, Yamunotri is the source of the Yamuna River. The Yamunotri temple is dedicated to Goddess Yamuna, and it is a significant pilgrimage site for Hindus. Pilgrims trek to the temple and offer prayers to seek the blessings of the goddess.
Gangotri: Situated in the Uttarkashi district, Gangotri is the origin of the Ganges River. The Gangotri temple is dedicated to Goddess Ganga, and it holds immense religious importance. Pilgrims visit to take a dip in the holy Ganges and perform rituals to purify themselves.
Kedarnath: Nestled in the Rudraprayag district, Kedarnath is dedicated to Lord Shiva. The Kedarnath temple is one of the twelve Jyotirlingas (sacred shrines of Lord Shiva) and is situated at a high altitude. Pilgrims often trek or use other means of transportation to reach Kedarnath and seek the blessings of Lord Shiva.
Badrinath: Located in the Chamoli district, Badrinath is dedicated to Lord Vishnu. The Badrinath temple is one of the Char Dham sites and is considered one of the holiest shrines of Vaishnavites. Pilgrims visit Badrinath to worship Lord Vishnu and participate in religious ceremonies.
The Char Dham Yatra is undertaken by devout Hindus, and completing the circuit is believed to cleanse the soul of sins and facilitate spiritual liberation (moksha). Pilgrims often embark on this journey during the summer months when the weather is conducive to travel in the high-altitude Himalayan region.

Day 1: Arrival in Dehradun or Haridwar
Pilgrims arrive in Dehradun or Haridwar, two major gateway cities to Uttarakhand.
Attend an orientation session about the Char Dham Yatra and its significance.
Rest and prepare for the journey ahead.

Day 2: Dehradun/Haridwar to Yamunotri
Depart for Yamunotri from Dehradun or Haridwar.
Drive to Hanuman Chatti and commence the trek to Yamunotri.
Visit the Yamunotri temple and perform rituals.
Overnight stay in Yamunotri or nearby location.

Day 3: Yamunotri to Gangotri Drive to Gangotri via Uttarkashi.
Visit the Gangotri temple dedicated to Goddess Ganga.
Attend the evening aarti (ritual of worship) at the temple.
Overnight stay in Gangotri or nearby location.

Day 4: Gangotri to Kedarnath
Drive to Kedarnath via Guptakashi.
Trek or use other means to reach Kedarnath.
Visit the Kedarnath temple, one of the twelve Jyotirlingas.
Attend the evening aarti at the temple.
Overnight stay in Kedarnath or nearby location.

Day 5: Kedarnath to Badrinath
Depart for Badrinath, traveling via Joshimath.
Visit the Badrinath temple dedicated to Lord Vishnu.
Attend the evening aarti at the temple.
Overnight stay in Badrinath or nearby location.

Day 6: Badrinath to Rudraprayag
Depart for Rudraprayag, a town situated at the confluence of Alaknanda and Mandakini rivers.
Visit the confluence (sangam) and other local attractions.
Overnight stay in Rudraprayag or nearby location.

Day 7: Rudraprayag to Rishikesh/Dehradun
Drive back to Rishikesh or Dehradun.
Visit temples and ghats along the way.
Conclude the Char Dham Yatra with a sense of spiritual fulfillment.

Note: The actual itinerary may vary depending on factors such as weather conditions, road accessibility, and individual preferences. Pilgrims are advised to check with tour operators for the most up-to-date information and to ensure a safe and comfortable journey. The Char Dham Yatra typically takes about 10 to 12 days, allowing for travel and rest days in addition to the visitation days at each sacred site.

Golden Temple
click here to Ask Price

Visit to the Golden Temple: The centerpiece of the tour is a visit to the Golden Temple itself. Pilgrims partake in the Darbar Sahib experience, which involves paying respects, listening to Gurbani (holy hymns), and participating in the prayer ceremonies.
Langar Experience: The tour often includes a visit to the community kitchen or Langar at the Golden Temple. Pilgrims may participate in the serving or partaking of the free communal meal, symbolizing equality and selfless service.
Akal Takht and Sikh Museum: The package may involve a visit to the Akal Takht, the highest temporal seat of Sikh authority, located within the Golden Temple complex. Pilgrims might explore the Sikh Museum, providing insights into the history and philosophy of Sikhism.
Wagah Border Ceremony: Some Golden Temple Tour packages include a trip to the Wagah Border, where the daily flag-lowering ceremony takes place. This vibrant event symbolizes the closing of the India-Pakistan border.
Amritsar City Exploration: Pilgrims may have the opportunity to explore other significant Sikh and historical sites in Amritsar, such as the historic Jallianwala Bagh.
Accommodation: The package often includes accommodation in close proximity to the Golden Temple, allowing pilgrims convenient access to the sacred site.
Transportation: The tour package typically includes transportation to and from the Golden Temple and other points of interest in Amritsar.
Guided Tours: Knowledgeable guides may accompany the pilgrims, providing insights into the history, significance, and rituals associated with the Golden Temple.
Spiritual Talks and Discourses: Some packages may incorporate spiritual talks or discourses by Sikh scholars or leaders, enhancing the pilgrims' understanding of Sikh philosophy and teachings.
Cultural Experiences: Pilgrims might be offered opportunities to experience Sikh music, art, and cultural practices during their visit to the Golden Temple.

Day 1: Arrival in Amritsar
Morning: Arrival and Check-in
Arrive in Amritsar and check into your accommodation.
Afternoon: Visit Jallianwala Bagh
Visit Jallianwala Bagh, a historical site related to the tragic Jallianwala Bagh massacre.
Spend some time understanding the historical significance of the place.
Evening: Wagah Border Ceremony
Attend the Wagah Border Ceremony, a vibrant and patriotic event at the India-Pakistan border.
Return to Amritsar for the night.

Day 2: Golden Temple Complex
Morning: Explore the Golden Temple Complex
Begin the day with a visit to the Golden Temple, Harmandir Sahib.
Participate in the morning prayers and experience the spiritual atmosphere.
Explore the architecture and beauty of the Golden Temple and its surroundings.
Afternoon: Langar and Volunteer Work
Partake in the langar (community kitchen) at the Golden Temple.
Consider volunteering in the langar, a unique experience where visitors can contribute to the serving of free meals.
Evening: Attend the Night Ceremony
Attend the night ceremony at the Golden Temple.
Experience the serene atmosphere and the illumination of the Golden Temple in the evening.

Day 3: Cultural and Historical Exploration
Morning: Visit Akal Takht and Sikh Museum
Explore Akal Takht, one of the five seats of temporal authority in Sikhism.
Visit the Sikh Museum within the Golden Temple complex.
Afternoon: Explore Amritsar
Visit local markets and shops around Amritsar.
Explore historical sites like Maharaja Ranjit Singh Museum and Ram Bagh Gardens.
Evening: Return and Reflection
Return to the Golden Temple for a peaceful evening.
Reflect on your experiences and spend some quiet time in the spiritual ambiance.

Day 4: Departure
Morning: Final Visit to Golden Temple
Take a final visit to the Golden Temple for morning prayers and peaceful contemplation.
Afternoon: Departure
Check out from your accommodation.
Depart from Amritsar, carrying the spiritual and cultural experiences of the Golden Temple.

Vaishno Devi Temple

Trek to the Vaishno Devi Temple: The central element of the package is the trek to the Vaishno Devi temple. Pilgrims can choose to walk the entire distance or use alternative means such as ponies, palkis (palanquins), or helicopters for part of the journey.
Darshan (Temple Visit): The package ensures pilgrims get the opportunity for darshan (viewing) of the holy cave where the shrine of Vaishno Devi is located. Pilgrims offer prayers and seek blessings from the goddess.
Accommodation: The package may include accommodation options in Katra, the base camp for the Vaishno Devi Yatra. This allows pilgrims convenient access to the starting point of the trek.
Helicopter Services: Some packages offer helicopter services to transport pilgrims from Katra to Sanjichhat, reducing the trekking distance and time.
Pooja and Prasad: Pilgrims may be provided with special arrangements for performing pujas (rituals) at the temple, and they may receive prasad (blessed food) as part of the religious experience.
Guided Tours: Knowledgeable guides may accompany pilgrims, providing information about the religious significance of the pilgrimage, the history of the temple, and the rituals associated with the Yatra.
Medical Facilities: Considering the challenging terrain, some packages include medical facilities and assistance for pilgrims in case of any health issues during the Yatra.
Catering and Meals: The package may include arrangements for meals, ensuring that pilgrims have access to nourishing food during their journey.
Yatra Registration and Permits: Pilgrims are often assisted with the necessary registrations and permits required for the Vaishno Devi Yatra.
Cultural and Spiritual Programs: Some packages may include cultural and spiritual programs, such as bhajan (devotional song) sessions or discourses, enhancing the overall religious experience.

Day 1: Arrival in Katra
Morning: Arrival in Katra
Arrive in Katra, the base camp for the Vaishno Devi Yatra.
Check into your accommodation.
Afternoon: Orientation and Registration
Attend an orientation session about the Yatra and its significance.
Complete any necessary registrations for the pilgrimage.
Evening: Rest and Preparation

Rest and prepare for the trek to Vaishno Devi.
Day 2: Katra to Vaishno Devi
Morning: Start of the Trek
Begin the trek to Vaishno Devi early in the morning.
The trek is approximately 13 kilometers and can be covered on foot, by ponies, or by palanquins.
Afternoon: Darshan at Vaishno Devi Temple
Reach the Vaishno Devi temple and have darshan (viewing) of the holy cave.
Perform rituals and seek blessings from Goddess Vaishno Devi.
Evening: Return to Katra
Begin the descent back to Katra after darshan.
Return to your accommodation in Katra.

Day 3: Leisure Day in Katra
Morning: Rest and Relaxation
Take a leisurely morning to rest and recover from the trek.
Afternoon: Local Sightseeing
Explore local markets and attractions in Katra.
Visit places of interest, if time permits.
Evening: Cultural Programs
Attend cultural programs or bhajan sessions organized in Katra.
Spend the evening in a peaceful and spiritual atmosphere.

Day 4: Departure from Katra
Morning: Final Visit to the Temple
Optionally, visit the Vaishno Devi temple once more for additional prayers.
Spend some quiet time in reflection.
Afternoon: Check-out and Departure
Check out from your accommodation.
Depart from Katra, concluding the Vaishno Devi Yatra.
