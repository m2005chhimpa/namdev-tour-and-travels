Hindu Pilgrimage · Uttarakhand Himalayas

Char Dham Yatra

The Char Dham Yatra — "four abodes" — takes devotees through four sacred shrines in the Garhwal Himalayas: Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath, and Badrinath. We start by picking you up from Haridwar or Dehradun and move through each shrine in the traditional order, with acclimatization built in for the high-altitude stretches.

10 DaysDuration
4 ShrinesPlaces covered
ModerateDifficulty
May – JunBest time

For most pilgrims this is a once-in-a-lifetime act of devotion. We keep the pace unhurried at each shrine and handle every permit and high-altitude logistic, so the journey stays focused on faith, not paperwork.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Pick-up from Haridwar/Dehradun, drive to Hanuman Chatti, then trek or pony ride to Yamunotri Temple.

Scenic drive through the Bhagirathi valley to Gangotri, origin of the sacred Ganga river.

Drive to Gaurikund, then trek (or helicopter option) to Kedarnath Temple, one of the twelve Jyotirlingas.

Drive via Joshimath to Badrinath, dedicated to Lord Vishnu, including the Tapt Kund hot springs.

Drive back to Haridwar/Dehradun with drop-off, completing the full circuit.

Places You'll Visit

Yamunotri

Source of the Yamuna, known for hot springs and remote setting.

Gangotri

Spiritual source of the Ganga, framed by glacial peaks.

Kedarnath

One of the twelve Jyotirlingas, perched among snowy peaks.

Inclusions & Exclusions

Included

  • AC vehicle for the full circuit
  • Hotel/guesthouse stays
  • Daily breakfast & dinner
  • Guide & permits

Not Included

  • Pony/palki/helicopter charges
  • Personal expenses
  • Travel insurance

Sikh Pilgrimage · Punjab

Golden Temple Tour

Centered on the Harmandir Sahib in Amritsar — the holiest shrine in Sikhism. We take you through the temple complex with time for darshan and the langar, and round out the trip with the Wagah Border ceremony and Jallianwala Bagh.

3 DaysDuration
4 SitesPlaces covered
EasyDifficulty
Year-roundBest time

The langar alone serves free meals to over 100,000 people a day. Combined with Jallianwala Bagh and the Wagah Border ceremony, this short trip carries real depth for its length.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Arrive, check in, visit the Harmandir Sahib in the evening for the illuminated temple and langar.

Morning visit to Jallianwala Bagh, evening flag-lowering ceremony at Wagah Border.

Optional early visit to the Palki Sahib ceremony, breakfast, then drop-off.

Places You'll Visit

Harmandir Sahib

The holiest Sikh shrine, famous for its gold sanctum and Sarovar.

Wagah Border

The daily flag-lowering ceremony, full of energy and pride.

Jallianwala Bagh

A memorial marking a pivotal moment in India's freedom struggle.

Inclusions & Exclusions

Included

  • AC vehicle for sightseeing
  • 2 nights hotel
  • Daily breakfast

Not Included

  • Lunch & dinner (except langar)
  • Personal expenses

Hindu Pilgrimage · Jammu & Kashmir

Vaishno Devi Yatra

A journey to the sacred cave shrine of Goddess Vaishno Devi in the Trikuta Mountains above Katra. We transfer you from Jammu to Katra and arrange the trek up to the Bhawan for darshan.

2-3 DaysDuration
3 SitesPlaces covered
ModerateDifficulty
Mar – OctBest time

The 13 km trek from Katra to the Bhawan is part of the experience — well-paved, with free langars along the way. We handle your registration slip and pony bookings in advance.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Pick-up from Jammu, drive to Katra, collect yatra registration slip.

Early trek (or pony/palki) to the Bhawan, darshan, optional Bhairon Temple visit.

Descend to Katra, transfer back to Jammu for onward travel.

Places You'll Visit

The Bhawan

The main cave shrine — the heart of the pilgrimage.

Katra

The base town, full of markets and pilgrim guesthouses.

Bhairon Temple

Traditionally visited after the Bhawan for a complete yatra.

Inclusions & Exclusions

Included

  • Jammu–Katra–Jammu transfers
  • Hotel in Katra
  • Registration assistance

Not Included

  • Pony/palki/battery car
  • Helicopter tickets

Hindu Pilgrimage · Andhra Pradesh

Tirupati Balaji Darshan

A visit to the Sri Venkateswara Temple atop the Tirumala hills — one of the world's most-visited pilgrimage sites. We arrange your Seva/darshan slot in advance, transfer you up the hill from Tirupati town, and include time at nearby temples.

2 DaysDuration
3 SitesPlaces covered
EasyDifficulty
Year-roundBest time

Tirumala sees tens of millions of pilgrims a year, and the queue system can be overwhelming to navigate alone. Pre-arranging your Seva slot is the single biggest thing that makes this trip smooth rather than stressful.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Arrive in Tirupati, drive up to Tirumala hills, check in, evening visit to the temple complex.

Early morning darshan at Sri Venkateswara Temple, visit Padmavathi Temple in Tiruchanur, descend to Tirupati for departure.

Places You'll Visit

Sri Venkateswara Temple

The main shrine atop Tirumala hills, one of the richest temples in the world.

Padmavathi Temple, Tiruchanur

Dedicated to Goddess Padmavathi, traditionally visited alongside Tirumala.

Tirupati Town

The base town at the foot of the hills, with markets and additional temples.

Inclusions & Exclusions

Included

  • Seva/darshan slot booking assistance
  • Tirupati–Tirumala transfers
  • 1 night hotel

Not Included

  • Meals
  • Special entry donation tickets

Hindu Pilgrimage · Uttar Pradesh

Kashi Vishwanath Yatra

Varanasi, one of the world's oldest continuously inhabited cities, centers this pilgrimage around the Kashi Vishwanath Temple — one of the twelve Jyotirlingas — alongside the Ganga Aarti at the ghats and a sunrise boat ride.

3 DaysDuration
4 SitesPlaces covered
EasyDifficulty
Oct – MarBest time

Watching the Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat at dusk, then returning at dawn for a boat ride as the city wakes along the river, is an experience many travelers describe as unlike anywhere else in India.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Arrive, check in, evening visit to Dashashwamedh Ghat for the Ganga Aarti ceremony.

Early morning boat ride along the ghats, followed by darshan at the Kashi Vishwanath Temple.

Half-day trip to Sarnath, where Buddha delivered his first sermon, before departure.

Places You'll Visit

The Ghats

Iconic riverfront steps along the Ganga, the heart of daily ritual in Varanasi.

Kashi Vishwanath Temple

One of the twelve Jyotirlingas, dedicated to Lord Shiva.

Sarnath

Where Buddha gave his first teaching after enlightenment.

Inclusions & Exclusions

Included

  • 2 nights hotel
  • Boat ride
  • Local sightseeing vehicle

Not Included

  • Meals
  • Special darshan tickets

Experience the ghats, the Aarti, and the temple, all in one trip.

Hindu Pilgrimage · Gujarat

Somnath & Dwarka Yatra

This coastal Gujarat circuit covers two of Hinduism's most significant sites: Somnath, first among the twelve Jyotirlingas, and Dwarka, the legendary kingdom of Lord Krishna. We connect both by road along the Arabian Sea coastline.

4 DaysDuration
3 SitesPlaces covered
EasyDifficulty
Oct – MarBest time

Somnath's evening sound-and-light show, telling the temple's story of destruction and rebuilding through the centuries, adds a layer of history to the spiritual visit that few other Jyotirlingas offer.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Arrive, check in, evening darshan at Somnath Temple followed by the sound-and-light show.

Scenic coastal drive to Dwarka, check in, evening Aarti at Dwarkadhish Temple.

Boat trip to Bet Dwarka island, visit to Rukmini Devi Temple.

Morning at leisure, transfer for onward travel.

Places You'll Visit

Somnath Temple

First among the twelve Jyotirlingas, on the Arabian Sea coast.

Dwarkadhish Temple

Dedicated to Lord Krishna, at the legendary site of his kingdom.

Bet Dwarka

An island shrine reachable by boat, believed to be Krishna's residence.

Inclusions & Exclusions

Included

  • AC vehicle, Somnath–Dwarka circuit
  • 3 nights hotel
  • Boat ride to Bet Dwarka

Not Included

  • Meals
  • Personal expenses

Ready for Somnath & Dwarka?

Two of Gujarat's most significant temples, one seamless trip.

Hindu Pilgrimage · Tamil Nadu

Rameswaram Yatra

Rameswaram, home to the Ramanathaswamy Temple and its famous corridor of pillars, is one of the twelve Jyotirlingas and part of the Char Dham (all-India) pilgrimage circuit. The island town also holds deep significance in the Ramayana.

2 DaysDuration
3 SitesPlaces covered
EasyDifficulty
Oct – AprBest time

The temple's corridor is the longest of any Hindu temple in India, and the ritual bath at 22 sacred wells (Theerthams) inside the complex is a tradition unique to Rameswaram among India's pilgrimage sites.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Arrive, check in, darshan and the sacred well ritual at Ramanathaswamy Temple.

Morning excursion to Dhanushkodi, the ghost town at the tip of the island, then depart.

Places You'll Visit

Ramanathaswamy Temple

One of the twelve Jyotirlingas, famed for its 1000-pillar corridor.

Dhanushkodi

A dramatic, deserted stretch where the Bay of Bengal meets the Indian Ocean.

Pamban Bridge

The engineering marvel connecting Rameswaram island to the mainland.

Inclusions & Exclusions

Included

  • 1 night hotel
  • Local vehicle

Not Included

  • Meals
  • Sacred well ritual fee (paid on-site)

Ready for the Rameswaram Yatra?

One of India's most unique coastal pilgrimages.

Hindu Pilgrimage · Jammu & Kashmir

Amarnath Yatra

A high-altitude pilgrimage to the Amarnath cave, home to a naturally formed ice lingam dedicated to Lord Shiva. Undertaken only during the short summer yatra window, this is the most physically demanding journey we offer — and among the most revered.

5 DaysDuration
2 RoutesTrek options
ChallengingDifficulty
Jul – AugYatra window

The yatra requires a compulsory health certificate and registration well in advance, since the government limits daily pilgrim numbers. We handle both, and recommend arriving a day early in Jammu to acclimatize before the trek begins.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Arrive in Jammu, complete health check-in and yatra registration formalities.

Drive to Pahalgam or Baltal (your chosen route base), rest and prepare for the trek.

Multi-day trek (or pony/helicopter option) to the Amarnath cave for darshan of the ice lingam.

Descend, transfer back to Jammu for onward travel.

Places You'll Visit

Amarnath Cave

Home to the naturally forming ice lingam, the focus of the pilgrimage.

Pahalgam

A scenic base town and one of two traditional trek starting points.

Baltal

The shorter, steeper alternate route to the cave shrine.

Inclusions & Exclusions

Included

  • Registration & health certificate assistance
  • Base camp accommodation
  • Jammu transfers

Not Included

  • Pony/palki/helicopter charges
  • Meals during trek
  • Travel & medical insurance (strongly recommended)

Sufi Pilgrimage · Rajasthan

Ajmer Sharif Dargah

The Dargah of Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti in Ajmer is one of the most visited Sufi shrines in the world, drawing pilgrims of all faiths. We combine your visit with nearby Pushkar, home to one of India's few Brahma temples.

2 DaysDuration
2 SitesPlaces covered
EasyDifficulty
Year-roundBest time

The Dargah's message of communal harmony draws Hindus, Muslims, and people of other faiths alike, especially during the annual Urs festival — a rare example of a shrine embraced across religious lines.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Arrive in Ajmer, check in, visit the Dargah of Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti for prayers.

Short drive to Pushkar, visit the Brahma Temple and Pushkar Lake, then depart.

Places You'll Visit

Ajmer Sharif Dargah

The shrine of Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti, revered across faiths.

Pushkar Lake

A sacred lake ringed by ghats, central to Pushkar's spiritual life.

Inclusions & Exclusions

Included

  • 1 night hotel
  • Ajmer–Pushkar transfers

Not Included

  • Meals
  • Offerings at the Dargah
