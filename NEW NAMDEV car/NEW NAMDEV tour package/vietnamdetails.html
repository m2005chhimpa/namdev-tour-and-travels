Classic Vietnam Tour
click here to Ask Price

Hanoi - Capital Exploration: The tour often starts in Hanoi, the capital city of Vietnam. Highlights include visits to the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum, One Pillar Pagoda, Temple of Literature, and the Old Quarter.
Halong Bay - Scenic Cruise: A cruise in Halong Bay, a UNESCO World Heritage site famous for its stunning limestone karsts. Activities may include exploring caves, kayaking, and experiencing the unique beauty of the bay.
Hue - Imperial Heritage: Exploration of the historic city of Hue, known for its imperial citadel, royal tombs, and Thien Mu Pagoda.
Hoi An - Ancient Town Charm: Visit to the charming ancient town of Hoi An, recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage site for its well-preserved architecture. Exploring the lantern-lit streets, traditional houses, and enjoying local cuisine.
Da Nang - Modernity and Tradition: Stop in Da Nang, a city that combines modernity with cultural richness. Sightseeing may include Marble Mountains, Dragon Bridge, and the Golden Bridge.
Ho Chi Minh City - Southern Hub: Arrival in Ho Chi Minh City (Saigon), exploring landmarks like the War Remnants Museum, Notre-Dame Cathedral Basilica of Saigon, and Ben Thanh Market.
Cultural Experiences: Inclusion of cultural activities like water puppet shows, cyclo rides, and traditional music performances.
Local Cuisine: Opportunities to savor Vietnamese cuisine through street food tours, local restaurants, and even cooking classes.
Mekong Delta - River Exploration: A trip to the Mekong Delta, offering a unique experience of river life, floating markets, and traditional villages.
Cu Chi Tunnels - Historical Insight: Exploration of the Cu Chi Tunnels, providing insights into the historical significance of the Vietnam War.
Relaxation and Exploration: Balanced itinerary allowing for both exploration of cultural and historical sites as well as moments of relaxation.

Day 1: Arrival in Hanoi
Arrive in Hanoi, the capital city of Vietnam.
Transfer to the hotel and check-in.
Welcome briefing and orientation.
Evening exploration of the Old Quarter with its vibrant streets and local markets.
Overnight in Hanoi.

Day 2: Hanoi City Tour
Visit the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum, One Pillar Pagoda, and the Temple of Literature.
Afternoon exploration of the Hoan Kiem Lake and Ngoc Son Temple.
Evening water puppet show.
Overnight in Hanoi.

Day 3: Halong Bay Cruise
Drive to Halong Bay.
Board a cruise for a full day exploring the bay, visiting caves, and enjoying activities like kayaking.
Sunset on the deck.
Overnight on the cruise.

Day 4: Halong Bay to Hanoi to Hue
Morning Tai Chi session on the cruise.
Explore more of Halong Bay.
Return to Hanoi and take a flight to Hue.
Transfer to the hotel in Hue.
Overnight in Hue.

Day 5: Hue Imperial City
Visit the Imperial City, including the Citadel and the Forbidden Purple City.
Explore the Thien Mu Pagoda and take a boat ride on the Perfume River.
Overnight in Hue.

Day 6: Hoi An Ancient Town
Drive to Hoi An, stopping at the Hai Van Pass for panoramic views.
Explore the charming ancient town of Hoi An.
Visit historical sites and enjoy the lantern-lit streets.
Overnight in Hoi An.

Day 7: Hoi An Cultural Experience
Morning visit to a local market and a cooking class.
Afternoon at leisure for shopping or optional activities.
Evening stroll along the Thu Bon River.
Overnight in Hoi An.

Day 8: Da Nang and Marble Mountains
Drive to Da Nang.
Explore the Marble Mountains, Non Nuoc Beach, and the My Khe Beach.
Visit the Dragon Bridge and the Lady Buddha statue.
Overnight in Da Nang.

Day 9: Flight to Ho Chi Minh City
Take a flight to Ho Chi Minh City (Saigon).
Explore the War Remnants Museum, Notre-Dame Cathedral Basilica of Saigon, and Ben Thanh Market.
Evening at leisure.
Overnight in Ho Chi Minh City.

Day 10: Cu Chi Tunnels and City Exploration
Morning visit to the Cu Chi Tunnels, exploring the historical underground network.
Afternoon city exploration, including the Reunification Palace and the Saigon Central Post Office.
Evening at leisure.
Overnight in Ho Chi Minh City.

Day 11: Mekong Delta Excursion
Day trip to the Mekong Delta, visiting My Tho and Ben Tre.
Explore traditional villages, enjoy a boat ride, and visit a local coconut candy workshop.
Return to Ho Chi Minh City in the evening.
Overnight in Ho Chi Minh City.

Day 12: Departure
Leisure time in the morning for last-minute shopping or exploration.
Check-out from the hotel.
Transfer to the airport for departure

Vietnam Adventure Tour

Trekking and Hiking: Exploration of Vietnam's mountainous regions, such as Sapa in the north or the Central Highlands. Guided treks through terraced rice fields, dense forests, and ethnic minority villages.
Motorbike Tours: Adventurous journeys on motorbikes through scenic landscapes, winding mountain roads, and coastal routes. Exploration of remote areas and hidden gems not easily accessible by traditional transportation.
Caving Expeditions: Visits to renowned cave systems, such as those in Phong Nha-Ke Bang National Park. Guided caving expeditions, exploring underground chambers and unique limestone formations.
Water Activities: Kayaking or canoeing in picturesque settings like Halong Bay or the Mekong Delta. White-water rafting adventures in rivers like the Da River or the Song Ma River.
Rock Climbing: Climbing adventures in locations such as the Marble Mountains near Da Nang. Professional guides and equipment provided for various skill levels.
Biking Adventures: Cycling tours through scenic countryside, coastal roads, or challenging mountain trails. Multi-day biking excursions with stops at local villages and cultural sites.
Zip-lining and High Ropes Courses: High-flying adventures with zip-lining experiences through forests or over scenic landscapes. High ropes courses offering a mix of challenges and stunning views.
Homestays and Cultural Immersion: Interactions with local communities and ethnic groups during trekking adventures. Overnight stays in homestays to experience traditional Vietnamese hospitality and way of life.
Adventure in National Parks: Exploration of Vietnam's national parks, such as Ba Be National Park or Cuc Phuong National Park. Guided tours to discover diverse flora and fauna, as well as opportunities for birdwatching and wildlife encounters.
Off-the-Beaten-Path Exploration: Visits to lesser-known destinations and remote areas, providing a sense of exploration and discovery. Opportunities for off-road adventures and exploration of hidden trails.
Cultural and Culinary Adventures: Immersion in local cultures, traditions, and festivals. Sampling local cuisine through market visits and cooking classes.

Coastal Destinations: The retreat typically includes stays in renowned coastal areas such as Phu Quoc, Nha Trang, Da Nang, or Mui Ne, known for their pristine beaches and clear waters.
Luxurious Accommodations: Accommodations often feature beachfront resorts, boutique hotels, or private villas offering stunning views of the sea. Upscale amenities, spa facilities, and personalized services contribute to a luxurious and comfortable stay.
Beach Activities: Opportunities for relaxing on the beach, sunbathing, and enjoying the warm tropical climate. Water activities such as snorkeling, scuba diving, paddleboarding, and jet skiing are often available.
Spa and Wellness: Spa and wellness services with rejuvenating treatments, massages, and yoga sessions. Wellness-focused activities to promote relaxation and rejuvenation.
Culinary Experiences: Indulgence in local and international cuisine at beachfront restaurants. Seafood-focused dining experiences with fresh catches from the local waters.
Exploration of Coastal Towns: Guided or self-guided exploration of nearby coastal towns to experience local culture, markets, and cuisine. Visits to fishing villages to understand the local way of life.
Island Escapes: Excursions to nearby islands for a secluded and intimate experience. Island hopping tours to explore different facets of the archipelago.
Sunset Cruises: Sunset cruises or boat tours for a romantic and scenic experience on the water. Opportunities for private charters or group excursions.
Relaxation and Leisure: Leisure time for reading, spa treatments, or simply enjoying the tranquility of the beach. Comfortable lounging areas and beachside activities at the resort.
Cultural Experiences: Cultural activities that complement the beach experience, such as traditional performances or cooking classes. Visits to local markets or historical sites for a well-rounded cultural retreat.
Tailored Itineraries: Flexibility in the itinerary to accommodate individual preferences, whether it's a more laid-back retreat or one filled with activities.
Romantic Getaways: Ideal for couples looking for a romantic escape, with options for private dinners on the beach, sunset picnics, and other intimate experiences.
