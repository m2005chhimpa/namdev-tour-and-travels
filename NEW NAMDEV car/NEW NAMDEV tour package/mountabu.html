The Mount Abu Experience

A refreshing hill retreat of lakes, granite landscapes, temples and peaceful forested viewpoints.

Mount Abu is Rajasthan's only hill station, rising in the Aravalli Range and offering a cooler mountain escape from the state's desert landscapes. From the serene waters of Nakki Lake and the intricate Dilwara Temples to Guru Shikhar, Sunset Point, Toad Rock, Achalgarh and peaceful nature parks, Mount Abu combines spirituality, scenery, heritage and relaxed hill-station charm.

Before You Go

Good to know

Best time to visit

October to March is especially comfortable for sightseeing. Summer is also popular because Mount Abu is cooler than much of Rajasthan, while monsoon can bring greener hills and misty scenery.

Getting there

Mount Abu is reached primarily by road from Abu Road, the nearest major rail gateway. It fits naturally into Rajasthan and Gujarat road-trip itineraries.

Getting around

A private cab is convenient for covering Guru Shikhar, Dilwara Temples, Achalgarh, viewpoints and nature spots in one sightseeing route. Central Nakki Lake areas are easy to explore on foot.

What to carry

  • Comfortable shoes for viewpoints, temple visits and walks.
  • A light jacket for cool mornings and evenings.
  • Sun protection, water and a light rain layer.
  • A camera or phone for lake, rock and mountain views.
