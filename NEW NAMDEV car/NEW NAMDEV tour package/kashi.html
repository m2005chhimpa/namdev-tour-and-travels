Travel Smart

Good to know

🗓️ Plan around timing

Temple, palace and attraction timings can change. Confirm current schedules before finalising your day.

🚗 Keep transfers simple

A private vehicle makes multi-stop sightseeing easier, especially when you want to combine major landmarks with quieter local stops.

📸 Leave room for moments

Don't over-pack the itinerary. Sunrise, sunset, local markets and unexpected streets often become the best memories.

🤝 Travel respectfully

Follow photography, dress, entry and local etiquette rules at temples and heritage sites.
