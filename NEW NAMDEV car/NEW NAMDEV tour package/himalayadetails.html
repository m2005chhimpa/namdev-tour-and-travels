Himalayan Heights Expedition
Ask For Price

Ancient Monasteries Exploration: Dive into the rich cultural heritage of Ladakh by exploring ancient monasteries such as Thiksey and Hemis. Marvel at the architectural beauty and spiritual ambiance that define these sacred Pangong Lake Marvel: Witness the surreal beauty of Pangong Lake, known for its ever-changing hues. Nestled between India and China, the lake's crystal-clear waters reflect the surrounding mountains, creating a picture-perfect landscape.
Nubra Valley Adventure: Explore the enchanting Nubra Valley, known for its stark desert landscape and picturesque villages. Visit Diskit Monastery, perched on a hill, and revel in the tranquility of this remote Himalayan region.
Cultural Immersion in Ladakh: Immerse yourself in Ladakhi culture through interactions with local communities. Experience the warmth of Ladakhi hospitality and gain insights into their traditional way of life.
Leh City Exploration: Discover the unique charm of Leh, the capital of Ladakh. Stroll through the bustling markets, explore the historic Leh Palace, and absorb the serene atmosphere of this high-altitude Himalayan town.
Scenic Accommodations: Stay in carefully selected accommodations, ranging from comfortable guesthouses to traditional Ladakhi homestays, providing an authentic and cozy Himalayan experience.
Expert-Guided Tours: Engage with knowledgeable local guides who share the stories, history, and spirituality associated with Ladakh's monasteries and landscapes.
High-Altitude Experience: Acclimate to the high-altitude environment with a gradual itinerary designed to ensure the well-being and comfort of travelers.
Culinary Delights: Sample Ladakhi cuisine, known for its hearty and flavorful dishes. Enjoy traditional meals that reflect the local flavors and culinary traditions of the region.
Comfortable Transportation: Travel in comfort between Ladakh's key destinations, allowing you to relax and enjoy the scenic beauty of the Himalayas.
Optional Add-Ons:
Camel safari in the sand dunes of Nubra Valley.
Hot air balloon ride over Pangong Lake for a unique perspective.
Attend a cultural performance or festival, if the itinerary coincides with local celebrations.


Duration: 10 Days/9 Nights

Day 1: Arrival in Leh
Arrive in Leh, the capital of Ladakh, and acclimate to the high-altitude environment.
Relax and take it easy to adjust to the altitude.
Evening orientation and welcome dinner.

Day 2: Leh City Exploration
Explore Leh's historic sites, including Leh Palace and Shanti Stupa.
Stroll through the local markets and soak in the unique atmosphere.
Optional visit to the Hall of Fame, a museum dedicated to the Indian armed forces.

Day 3: Thiksey Monastery and Shey Palace
Journey to Thiksey Monastery, a beautiful Gompa with stunning views of the Indus Valley.
Explore Shey Palace, the former summer retreat of the kings of Ladakh.
Return to Leh and spend the evening at leisure.

Day 4: Hemis Monastery and Pangong Lake
Drive to Hemis Monastery, one of the largest and wealthiest monasteries in Ladakh.
Continue the journey to the mesmerizing Pangong Lake.
Enjoy the breathtaking views and stay overnight by the lakeside.

Day 5: Pangong Lake Exploration
Explore the surroundings of Pangong Lake.
Optional activities include a boat ride or a short hike for panoramic views.
Experience the tranquility of this remote Himalayan gem.

Day 6: Nubra Valley via Khardung La Pass
Drive to Nubra Valley, crossing the world's highest motorable pass, Khardung La.
Visit Diskit Monastery and enjoy panoramic views of the Nubra Valley.
Explore Hunder Village and its sand dunes.

Day 7: Camel Safari and Turtuk Village
Enjoy a camel safari amidst the sand dunes of Hunder.
Visit the enchanting Turtuk Village, known for its apricot orchards and cultural richness.
Return to Leh in the evening.

Day 8: Sham Valley Exploration
Explore Sham Valley, known as the "Apricot Valley."
Visit Alchi Monastery and Likir Monastery, showcasing unique Tibetan-Buddhist architecture.
Return to Leh and spend the evening at leisure.

Day 9: Monastery Circuit - Stakna, Matho, and Stok
Explore lesser-known monasteries on the outskirts of Leh.
Visit Stakna Monastery, Matho Monastery, and Stok Monastery.

Gain insights into the spiritual and cultural aspects of Ladakh.
Day 10: Departure from Leh
Bid farewell to the mystical landscapes of Ladakh.
Depart from Leh with memories of the Himalayan Heights Expedition.

Tranquil Retreat in Shimla and Manali
Ask For Price

Comfortable Accommodations: Stay in serene and comfortable hotels, providing a peaceful retreat in the lap of nature.
Sightseeing Tours: Guided tours to major attractions in Shimla and Manali, ensuring you don't miss the cultural and natural highlights of each destination.
Adventure Options: Optional adventure activities in both Shimla and Manali, catering to the interests of adventure enthusiasts.
Local Cuisine Delights: Savor local Himachali cuisine at recommended restaurants, offering a taste of regional flavors.
Comfortable Transportation: Travel between Shimla and Manali in comfortable vehicles, ensuring a pleasant and scenic journey.

Optional Add-Ons:
River rafting in the Beas River.
Visit to Naggar Castle in Kullu.
Traditional Himachali cultural show in Manali.

Duration:7Days/6Nights
Day 1-2: Arrival in Shimla
Arrive in Shimla, the "Queen of Hills," and check into your tranquil accommodation.
Explore the Mall Road, visit Christ Church, and take a leisurely stroll in the lush greenery of the Ridge.
Optional: Enjoy a ride on the historic Shimla-Kalka toy train.

Day 3-4: Exploring Shimla's Surroundings
Excursion to Kufri, a charming hill station near Shimla, known for its scenic beauty and adventure activities.
Visit Jakhoo Temple, the highest point in Shimla, offering panoramic views of the Himalayas.
Explore Mashobra and Naldehra for their natural beauty and golf course.

Day 5-6: Tranquil Manali
Drive to Manali, passing through scenic landscapes and the Kullu Valley.
Explore Old Manali and the Manu Temple.
Visit Hadimba Devi Temple and stroll through the charming streets of Manali.
Optional: Excursion to Solang Valley for adventure activities like paragliding and zorbing.

Day 7: Departure
Depart from Manali with memories of a tranquil retreat in the Himalayas.

Spiritual Sojourn
Ask For Price

Spiritual Accommodations: Stay in serene and spiritually iclined accommodations, providing an atmosphere of tranquility.
Guided Spiritual Tours: Engage in guided tours to spiritual sites, with knowledgeable guides providing insights into the rich cultural and religious heritage of Haridwar and Rishikesh.
Participation in Rituals: Attend Ganga Aarti and other rituals in both Haridwar and Rishikesh, offering a firsthand experience of the spiritual practices.
Yoga and Meditation Opportunities: Optional yoga and meditation sessions to rejuvenate your mind and body.
Comfortable Transportation: Travel between Haridwar and Rishikesh in comfortable vehicles, ensuring a peaceful and enjoyable journey.
Optional Add-Ons:

Ayurvedic wellness experiences.
River rafting adventure in Rishikesh.
Trek to nearby spiritual caves or temples.
Duration: 5 Days/4 Nights

Day 1: Arrival in Haridwar
Arrive in Haridwar, one of the seven holiest places in Hinduism.
Check into your accommodation and experience the vibrant energy of the ghats along the Ganges.

Day 2: Haridwar's Spiritual Marvels
Attend the Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri, a mesmerizing ritual on the banks of the Ganges.
Explore other significant temples in Haridwar, including Mansa Devi Temple and Chandi Devi Temple.
Take a dip in the holy Ganges for spiritual purification.

Day 3: Haridwar to Rishikesh
Drive to Rishikesh, the "Yoga Capital of the World."
Visit Triveni Ghat for a spiritual experience and witness the evening Aarti.
Explore the vibrant market and attend a yoga or meditation session (optional).

Day 4: Rishikesh's Spiritual Tranquility
Visit the iconic Lakshman Jhula and Ram Jhula, two suspension bridges over the Ganges.
Explore the famous Beatles Ashram, known for its association with the Beatles during their visit to Rishikesh.
Attend an evening Aarti at Parmarth Niketan Ashram.

Day 5: Departure from Rishikesh
Take part in a morning yoga or meditation session (optional).
Depart from Rishikesh with a heart full of spiritual experiences.
