Before You Go

Good to know

Best time to visit

October to March is the most comfortable window — cooler days make it easier to cover forts like Amber and Nahargarh, which involve a fair bit of walking. Summers (April–June) get very hot; if you're travelling then, plan fort visits for early morning.

How to reach Jaipur

  • By air: Jaipur International Airport connects to most major Indian cities.
  • By train: Jaipur Junction is well-linked to Delhi, Mumbai, and across Rajasthan.
  • By road: A comfortable drive from Delhi or Agra — we can arrange the car for you, whichever direction you're coming from.
While You're Here

What to eat & where to shop

🍛

Dal Baati Churma

Jaipur's signature thali — baked wheat rolls with lentils and a sweet crumble.

🥟

Pyaaz Kachori

A flaky, spiced onion-stuffed pastry — best eaten fresh and hot from a local stall.

🍮

Ghewar

A honeycomb-textured sweet, especially popular during festival season.

🥤

Lassi

Thick, chilled, and often served in a kulhad — a clay cup you keep as a souvenir.

🧵

Bapu Bazaar

Textiles, jootis (traditional footwear), and block-printed fabric.

💍

Johari Bazaar

Jaipur's jewellery market — known for gemstones and traditional Rajasthani design.

🏺

Blue Pottery

A Jaipur specialty — distinctive cobalt-blue ceramics found across the old city's markets.
