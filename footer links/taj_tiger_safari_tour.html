Preparing your desert journey…

The Taj & Tiger Safari Tour brings together two very different sides of India: extraordinary Mughal heritage and the raw beauty of the Indian jungle.

Stand before the Taj Mahal in Agra, discover the royal architecture and colourful heritage of Jaipur, then head into Ranthambore for an exciting wildlife safari where the Bengal tiger is the star of the landscape.

The route can be customized with additional safari drives, heritage walks, local experiences, hotel upgrades and extra nights according to your travel dates and interests.

  • 01 Sunrise or daytime Taj Mahal experience in Agra
  • 02 Agra Fort and Mughal heritage sightseeing
  • 03 Ranthambore jungle safari with expert naturalist
  • 04 Tiger country, lakes, forests and historic ruins
  • 05 Jaipur's Amber Fort, City Palace and Pink City heritage
The Route

Six days of iconic heritage and wild India

A carefully paced journey combining the Taj Mahal, royal Rajasthan and thrilling wildlife safaris in Ranthambore.

Taj & Tiger Safari — 6 Days / 5 Nights PKG-TIGER-TAJ-01
Day 1

Arrival in Delhi → Agra — Gateway to the Taj

Pickup from Delhi airport, railway station or hotel and transfer to Agra. After check-in, explore Agra Fort and the city's Mughal heritage. Evening at leisure or optional sunset Taj Mahal viewpoint.

Delhi → Agra · Overnight
Day 2

Taj Mahal → Fatehpur Sikri → Ranthambore

Start with a memorable Taj Mahal visit, ideally around sunrise. Continue toward Ranthambore with an optional stop at Fatehpur Sikri. Arrive at the wildlife destination and relax at your resort.

Agra → Ranthambore · Overnight
Day 3

Ranthambore — Morning Tiger Safari

Early morning jeep or canter safari inside Ranthambore National Park. Explore forest, grassland and historic ruins with a trained naturalist. Afternoon at leisure with an optional second safari depending on availability.

Tiger Safari · Overnight
Day 4

Ranthambore — Second Safari → Jaipur

Enjoy another early safari for a different wildlife experience. Later drive toward Jaipur and check in. Evening free to explore the Pink City's markets or enjoy a traditional dinner.

Ranthambore → Jaipur · Overnight
Day 5

Amber Fort → City Palace → Pink City

Explore Amber Fort, Jal Mahal viewpoint, City Palace and selected heritage landmarks. Spend time in Jaipur's colourful bazaars for handicrafts, textiles and traditional souvenirs.

Royal Jaipur · Overnight
Day 6

Jaipur → Delhi / Departure

Enjoy a relaxed breakfast and optional morning sightseeing or shopping. Transfer to Delhi airport, railway station or your preferred departure point according to your travel plan.
