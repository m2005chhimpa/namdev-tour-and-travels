Jaipur rewards a single, well-paced day more than almost any city in Rajasthan — if the route is right.

This package strings together the city's essential monuments in the order they're best seen: Amber Fort while the light is still soft and the crowds thin, the pink sandstone façade of Hawa Mahal at a civil hour, and the marble courtyards of City Palace before the afternoon heat sets in. Your driver knows the entry lines, the parking, and which viewpoints are worth the extra five minutes.

Built for first-time visitors, families, and anyone with one day in Jaipur who wants to see it properly rather than quickly.

  • 01 Private AC vehicle with driver, fuel and parking included
  • 02 Route sequenced to avoid the midday sun at outdoor sites
  • 03 Flexible stops — skip or linger at any monument
  • 04 English/Hindi speaking driver; local guide available on request
  • 05 Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup
The Route

How the day unfolds

A suggested sequence — timings flex around your pickup hour and pace.

Jaipur Sightseeing — Day Pass PKG-JPR-01
8:00 AM

Amber Fort

Start early to beat the heat and the crowds. Walk the ramparts and courtyards of this hilltop fort, with time for photos over the Maota Lake below.

Allow 90 min
10:00 AM

Jal Mahal — photo stop

A short drive-by halt at the "Water Palace," floating at the centre of Man Sagar Lake. Best for photographs from the road.

Allow 15 min
11:00 AM

City Palace

Explore the courtyards, museums and gates of the royal residence, still partly home to Jaipur's former ruling family.

Allow 75 min
1:00 PM

Jantar Mantar

Walk among the giant stone astronomical instruments of this 18th-century observatory, right next door to City Palace.

Allow 45 min
2:15 PM

Lunch break

A stop at a local Rajasthani thali restaurant along the route (meal cost not included in the package price).

Allow 45 min
3:30 PM

Hawa Mahal & Old City Bazaar

View the honeycombed façade of the "Palace of Winds" from the street, then browse Johari Bazaar for lac bangles, textiles and gemstones before drop-off.

Allow 60 min
