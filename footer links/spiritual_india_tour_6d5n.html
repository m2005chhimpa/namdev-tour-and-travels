Preparing your desert journey…

Spiritual India is a journey of sacred places, living traditions and peaceful moments. From the devotional energy of Mathura and Vrindavan to the Ganga aarti of Haridwar and the yoga culture of Rishikesh, every stop offers a different way to experience India's spiritual heritage.

The journey combines temple visits, holy ghats, evening aarti, meditation, yoga and time for quiet reflection. It is designed at a comfortable pace so travelers can experience the atmosphere of each destination rather than simply ticking off sightseeing points.

The route can be customized with additional temple visits, ashram stays, yoga sessions, guided heritage walks, meditation experiences and extra nights according to your travel dates and interests.

  • 01 Mathura & Vrindavan — Krishna devotion and sacred temples
  • 02 Haridwar — Ganga ghats and evening Ganga Aarti
  • 03 Rishikesh — yoga, meditation and riverside serenity
  • 04 Sacred temples, ghats and traditional rituals
  • 05 Private vehicle, flexible sightseeing and peaceful stays
The Sacred Route

6 days through India's spiritual heartland

A peaceful journey through Krishna's land, the sacred Ganga and the yoga capital of the world.

Spiritual India — 6 Days / 5 Nights PKG-SPIRIT-IND-01
Day 1

Arrival in Delhi → Mathura & Vrindavan

Pickup from Delhi airport, railway station or hotel and drive toward Mathura. Visit selected sacred sites in Mathura and continue to Vrindavan. Evening temple visit and devotional atmosphere before returning to the hotel.

Delhi → Mathura / Vrindavan · Overnight
Day 2

Vrindavan Temple Trail → Haridwar

Continue exploring Vrindavan's famous temples and sacred lanes. Later drive toward Haridwar and settle into your hotel. In the evening, experience the spiritual atmosphere of Har Ki Pauri and attend Ganga Aarti according to the day's schedule.

Vrindavan → Haridwar · Overnight
Day 3

Haridwar — Ganga Ghats & Sacred Temples

Explore Haridwar's important temples and riverside ghats. Spend time beside the Ganga for prayer, reflection or a peaceful walk. Evening Ganga Aarti offers one of the journey's most memorable spiritual experiences.

Haridwar · Ganga Aarti · Overnight
Day 4

Haridwar → Rishikesh — Yoga & Ashram Culture

Short transfer to Rishikesh. Visit the riverside areas around Ram Jhula and Laxman Jhula, explore ashram districts and enjoy time beside the Ganga. Optional yoga or meditation session can be arranged.

Rishikesh · Yoga & Meditation · Overnight
Day 5

Rishikesh — Spiritual Morning → Delhi

Begin with an optional sunrise yoga or meditation session. Explore peaceful riverside locations and spiritual centres before returning toward Delhi. Evening at leisure after hotel check-in.

Rishikesh → Delhi · Overnight
Day 6

Delhi → Departure

Enjoy breakfast and optional time for a quiet morning or final shopping before your private transfer to Delhi airport, railway station or your preferred departure point.

Tour Ends
